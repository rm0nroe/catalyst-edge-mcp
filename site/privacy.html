Privacy notice

The local MCP server and this website have separate data boundaries. The local server keeps ticker requests and evidence on your machine. Its default discovery collector downloads bounded public GDELT dataset files and retains publisher titles, links, hashes, and derived entity decisions—not article bodies. The website collects only the limited demand-test data described below.

Site measurement

A random browser identifier, referral source, and exposure, install-click, or self-reported activation event may be stored. The server stores a keyed hash of the browser identifier, not the identifier itself.

Hosted Pro interest

If you opt in, Catalyst Edge stores your encrypted email, a keyed email hash, selected MCP client, referral source, consent and confirmation timestamps, and whether your response was linked to a self-reported activation.

Not collected

The website does not receive your tickers, prompts, portfolio, brokerage credentials, local SQLite evidence, or MCP tool results. IP addresses are used only for abuse-rate limiting and are stored as keyed hashes for no more than two days.

Retention and deletion

Unconfirmed requests expire after 24 hours. Rate-limit records expire after two days. All other demand-test records expire after 180 days. Request earlier deletion at rmonroe128+catalyst-edge@gmail.com from the same address you submitted.
