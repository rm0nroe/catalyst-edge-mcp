# Evidence Terminal

Evidence Terminal presents Catalyst Edge as a high-end research surface for analysts,
builders, and technically capable individual investors. The design begins with the product's
actual job: turn one ticker request into a compact, source-linked dossier while keeping every
coverage boundary visible. It borrows the clarity and information discipline of professional
trading workstations without implying execution, portfolio access, real-time prices, or a
forecasting model.

The signature visual is the evidence trace. A ticker command anchors the surface; source
events appear in chronological order; typed family states sit in a coverage matrix; and the
bounded dossier resolves at the end. Connectors exist only inside reserved gutters, never
behind or across content. The viewer can read the system left to right without decoding an
abstract metaphor.

The material language is graphite glass over a disciplined market grid. Deep navy-black
surfaces create terminal depth, cyan identifies active research paths, mint marks verified
observations, amber marks explicit coverage boundaries, and violet marks reverse provenance
readback. Color always appears with a text label and distinct container treatment. No color
represents bullish or bearish market direction.

Typography behaves like a modern financial platform: a restrained sans serif for promises
and hierarchy, tabular monospace for tickers, dates, tools, states, and scores. Large type is
used only for the audience promise and result state. The supporting interface is dense but
quiet, with crisp one-pixel rules, stable spacing, and shallow depth instead of decorative
neon, floating icon badges, or generic sci-fi HUD ornament.

The six artifacts operate as one campaign system while giving each format a distinct job.
The primary hero sells market-speed research with source-level proof; the tools hero explains
the exact forward and reverse MCP paths; the coverage hero makes missingness legible; the
social crops make the proof promise memorable; and the AAPL one-pager maps real request fields
to the five canonical families, bounded result, and provenance readback.

The emotional promise is control at research speed. Catalyst Edge should feel like a new
piece of analyst infrastructure—clean, technical, and unusually honest about what it knows—
not a trading signal service, a brokerage screen, or a generic campaign template.

## Visual principles

- The actual product workflow is the hero.
- Connectors stay in dedicated gutters and never cross labels or panels.
- One dominant analytical surface per composition; supporting data remains subordinate.
- Verified, boundary, neutral, and provenance states always pair color with exact text.
- No fabricated market prices, price charts, claim IDs, accession numbers, hashes, or URLs.
- No bullish/bearish semantics for missing data; unavailable states contribute zero direction.
- Every export must be inspected at full size, not only validated by dimensions or contrast.
