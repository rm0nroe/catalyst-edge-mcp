Catalyst Edge has no permanent logo, color, type, or UI token source of truth outside the
superseded launch packages. The new campaign system is **Evidence Terminal**: a dark,
finance-native research workstation for analysts, builders, and technically capable individual
investors. Its signature visual is the evidence trace—ticker request, chronological source
events, five exact family states, bounded deterministic dossier, and reverse provenance
readback. Connectors are confined to reserved gutters and never cross text or panels. Cyan
marks active research paths, mint marks verified observations, amber marks explicit coverage
boundaries, and violet marks source readback; every color is paired with exact text and no
color represents bullish or bearish direction. The small waveform tile is campaign-scoped,
not a permanent logo decision.

## 60–90 second script

Catalyst Edge turns recent public-company evidence into a source-linked research
dossier. Here is a fixed AAPL example using the public SEC-only default.

For the 14 days ending August 3, 2026, Catalyst Edge found two primary-source records:
Apple's July 31 Form 10-Q and its July 30 Form 8-K reporting results of operations and
financial condition. The dossier links directly to both SEC filings and preserves their
accession numbers and retrieval timestamps.

The result is neutral at 50 with partial coverage. That is not a trading signal. Both
items are neutral filing events. Direct SEC insider activity was queried but returned no
qualifying observation. Bluesky partial public attention was disabled by the public
package default.
Options flow and technical data require rights-cleared licensed sources. Catalyst Edge
shows those distinct states instead of converting absent data into bearish evidence.

The current `rules_v1` scoring method is deterministic and unbacktested; it is not a
trained predictive model. A separate experimental trained scorer is not production
approved and is not part of the Local Beta claim. This creative package remains
human-review-only. The output does not provide an investment recommendation. The next
step is to open the linked SEC filings, inspect the filed facts and exhibits, and check
whether broader market or sector events explain the observation.
